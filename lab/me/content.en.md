## What I built

**[me.yunmin.dev](https://me.yunmin.dev)** is my CV page. If yunmin.dev is a workshop where I put whatever I like, me is the page that introduces me briefly and precisely.

## What's on it

- **Highlights:** big number cards for 1st at the AIKU Juniorthon, top 4% at LG Aimers, and the Excellence Award at the science fair
- **Sections:** Education, Competitions, Research, Projects, Activities, Skills
- Competition and research entries link straight to the matching project pages on yunmin.dev.

## Design

It follows the yunmin.dev look (ivory wall, colored holds), but it's kept **short and compact** because it's a CV. Each section gets a number and a color in order, and printing it from the browser gives a clean one-page PDF.

## How it's built

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, deployed on Cloudflare Workers. Editing one `data.js` file updates the page.
