## Overview

I'm taking part in the **DAB (Data Analytics for Business) Competition**, run by the Center for Digital Transformation & Business (CDTB) at Korea University Business School. Teams analyze public or company data to come up with **new business model ideas**.

Our team, **한롯기두키**, chose **a dynamic pricing algorithm for the KBO, built for the Korean market**. We made the finals, which guarantees us **either the grand prize or an excellence award**.

## How the competition runs

- Teams of 3 to 5, running for about five months from June to November 2026.
- An **interim review** in August (10-minute talk plus Q&A), then a **final presentation video** in October, after which the finalists are picked.
- The **finals** (November 6, DT Day) are live presentations, with one grand prize and two excellence awards.
